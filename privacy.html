Privacy Policy

Subvanta — last updated 1 August 2026

Subvanta does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. There is no account, no sign-up, and no analytics. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do.

What Subvanta stores

Everything you enter — subscription names, prices, billing cycles, renewal dates, categories, notes, cancellation links, and any images you choose — is stored only in the app's private database on your device. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that hold your data and no way to see it.

What Subvanta does not do

  • No user accounts, logins, or sign-ups
  • No connection to your bank, card, or Apple Account
  • No analytics, crash reporting, tracking, advertising, or third-party SDKs
  • No sale or sharing of personal data — nothing leaves your device to share
  • No access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera roll (beyond an image you explicitly pick), or browsing history

Network connections

Subvanta makes only two kinds of network request, and neither one sends your data:

  • Exchange rates. When you track subscriptions in more than one currency, the app downloads a public exchange-rate table from open.er-api.com so it can show a single combined total. The request contains only a currency code, such as USD. It contains nothing about your subscriptions, and all conversion happens on your device.
  • Service icons (off by default). If you turn on "Fetch service icons" in Settings, the app requests the icon file directly from each service's own website — for example netflix.com/apple-touch-icon.png. That tells the service only that some device asked for its public icon. This is off unless you enable it, and you can instead set your own image for each subscription.

Notifications

Renewal reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device by iOS. They are never sent through a server, and no push tokens are collected.

Purchases

Subvanta Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; Subvanta never sees your payment details, card number, or Apple Account credentials. The app only asks iOS whether the unlock has been purchased.

Exporting your data

You can export all of your subscriptions to a CSV file at any time from Settings. That file goes wherever you choose to send it — Files, iCloud Drive, or another app. Once you share it, this policy no longer governs it, because it is then in the hands of whatever service you sent it to.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its database and every subscription in it. Because nothing is stored off your device, there is no server-side copy to request or erase. Any CSV files you exported yourself remain wherever you saved them.

Children

Subvanta is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, regardless of age.
